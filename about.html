Our Story

The Smoke & Oak Steakhouse Experience

Opened in 2018, Smoke & Oak was born from a simple belief: great steak doesn't need complicated. We dry-age our beef in-house for a minimum of 28 days, hand-cut every portion, and grill over a live oak fire that we feed throughout every service. The result is a depth of flavor that can't be replicated.

Our bar program is equally serious. We barrel-age our own cocktails, carry over 200 whiskeys, and our sommelier curates a wine list that pairs perfectly with every cut on the menu. Whether you are celebrating an anniversary or closing a deal, we make every meal an event.
